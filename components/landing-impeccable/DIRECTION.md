Development-only. Never copy into code, markup or anything the browser receives.

Run mode: the Impeccable launcher was not run (it downloads a binary; no owner's yes in the conversation) — the skill's
«Launcher unavailable» path: no concept-seed roll, no comps, no sub-agent reviewer; code-led build, one self review pass.

THESIS: The idea is a station and the element is the train that leaves it. The page refuses the category default of an AI
landing (dark mesh, glowing gradient headline, three feature cards) and speaks the grammar of a Swiss departure board.

OWN-WORLD: SBB-style station signage, married to the node's Design element (step 333-2, owner's decision «from the
existing tokens»): the board, the station sign and the closing band are `--primary`; the departure poster, the ticker and
the departure time are `--accent`; the one action is `--destructive`; the hall floor, the ticket and the text are the
neutrals (`--muted`, `--background`, `--foreground`, `--border`); display type is `--font-heading`, reading type
`--font-body`, corners `--radius`. No colour, face or radius lives in the widget: changing the Design changes the board,
light and dark included. The role table: /code/development-docs/development-steps/new-steps/333-1-*.md.

STORY: The visitor reads «departing from: your idea» and three destinations (agent, application, automation), understands
that the element becomes one of them, sees what is included in the ticket, and boards: «Claude Code subscription».

FIRST VIEWPORT: a blue station-name sign across the top with the invitation headline and «on blockchain, and beyond»; under
it the departure board: origin row, three destination rows whose names flip in on load with their «via» line underneath,
platform numbers in white squares, «when you are ready» as the departure time; the claim with its «?» as the board's
ticker line; the red action and the blue outline action under the board.

FORM: departure board + timetable poster + ticket; position 1 of 7 on the list (board, timetable poster, ticket,
station clock, route map, platform sign, luggage tag); seed key: none (launcher not run).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every
